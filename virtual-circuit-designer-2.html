Welcome to the bench

Every part here is a real, buyable component with its real pins and values. Tap any part — on the shelf or on the board — to learn what it does before you wire it.

Try this first

1. Place a 9V battery, a 220 Ω resistor, and a red LED.
2. Wire battery + → resistor → LED long leg (+), LED short leg → battery −.
3. Press Power Check — the LED lights and the PDF unlocks.

💡 LEDs only work one way round. The long leg is + (anode). Always use a resistor with an LED, or it can burn out — this bench enforces that, just like real life.
